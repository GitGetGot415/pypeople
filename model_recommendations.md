# Ollama Model Recommendations

- `llama3` works well enough, but it's somewhat slow, and sometimes returns errors due to how long it takes.

- `qwen2.5:3b` is only 1.9 GB and much faster than any of the other models listed here, though the outputted text is somewhat low quality.

- `qwen2.5:7b` basically the same thing as qwen2.5:3b but slower. However, it does have ever so slightly higher quality output.
