# Ollama Model Recommendations

- `llama3` works well enough, but it's somewhat slow

- `qwen2.5:3b` is only 1.9 GB, and much faster, though the outputted text is rather low quality

- `qwen2.5:7b` is basically the same thing as qwen2.5:3b but with slightly higher quality output. Still not as good as llama3, though it is faster.
